Predicates, precision & degenerate shapes

Coordinates are integers from −10,000 to 10,000, with at most 500 input points. Orientation, hull area, and squared distances remain exact within JavaScript's safe integer range. Rectangle projections and lengths use floating-point arithmetic.

The hull drops collinear interior edge points. The diameter uses rotating calipers on that hull. The minimum-area enclosing rectangle checks each hull-edge orientation. Equal-area orientations can have several valid answers; the first within a small numerical tolerance is retained.

A single point has zero area, perimeter, and diameter. A line has zero area; its closed-boundary perimeter is twice its endpoint distance. Point-location queries return boundary for points on that segment, and outside otherwise. No interior is assigned to degenerate hulls.

The canvas rounds additions and probes to integer coordinates. It uses equal scale on both axes. All data and undo history stay in this browser; save before refreshing.
